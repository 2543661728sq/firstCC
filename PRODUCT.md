# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Portfolio visitors include graduate admissions reviewers, design mentors, hiring teams, collaborators, and clients who need to understand the designer's focus within the first 30 seconds. They usually scan quickly on desktop or mobile, looking for identity, strongest work, design thinking, and a direct way to contact.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio presents Sun Qi's product design work across industrial design, intelligent hardware, medical health, child products, public safety, and landscape experience. Success means visitors can quickly understand the designer's positioning, trust the quality of the work, open detailed case studies, and contact the designer without friction.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, humane. The site should feel like a thoughtful product design portfolio rather than a generic template: clear enough for fast scanning, warm enough to show care for users, and polished enough to support high-quality design work.

## Anti-references

Avoid template-like personal sites that rely on empty animation, vague slogans, hidden project information, oversized decorative cards, or weak mobile behavior. Avoid making the portfolio feel like a resume pasted onto a web page; the work, process, and product value should lead.

## Design Principles

- Make the 30-second read obvious: name, field, strongest work, and contact path must be immediately visible.
- Show work before decoration: visual polish should make the projects easier to understand, not compete with them.
- Turn project pages into case studies: each detail page should clarify challenge, role, design decisions, and value.
- Keep mobile first-impression strong: navigation, hero text, project cards, and contact must work cleanly on small screens.
- Preserve the designer's voice: use concise Chinese copy, real project imagery, and restrained motion to create confidence.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for readable contrast, tappable mobile targets, visible content without animation dependency, reduced-motion support, descriptive alt text, and clear keyboard/focus behavior where interaction exists.
